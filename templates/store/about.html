Frequently Asked Questions

What payment methods do you accept?
We accept Cash on Delivery (COD), Credit/Debit Cards (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay), and UPI options (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm).
How long does shipping take?
Standard delivery takes 3–5 business days. Express same-day or next-day shipping is available in selected metro cities.
What is your return policy?
We offer a hassle-free 30-day return policy. If you're not completely satisfied with your purchase, return it for a full refund or exchange.
How do I track my order?
Once your order is placed, you can track its status under "My Orders" in your account profile. We also send SMS and Email tracking updates.
